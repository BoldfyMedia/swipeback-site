Common questions

I deleted something by mistake.
Open the Photos app → Albums → Recently Deleted. Everything Swipeback deletes stays there for about 30 days.

How do I cancel Swipeback Plus?
On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Swipeback → Cancel. You can also tap "Manage subscription" in Swipeback's Settings. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

I got a new phone. How do I restore Plus?
Open Swipeback, go to Settings, and tap "Restore purchases".

Do you see my photos?
No. Nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy.

The widget is empty.
Open Swipeback once so it can fill the widget in.
