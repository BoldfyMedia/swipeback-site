Swipeback

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

The short version: Swipeback works entirely on your device. Your photos, screenshots and choices never leave it. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. The only thing the app sends is anonymous usage statistics (like "finished a Rewind"), which you can turn off in Settings. We never sell or share data.

Who we are

Swipeback ("the app", "we") is an app for iPhone and Android that helps you rediscover old screenshots and free up storage. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Your photos and screenshots

Swipeback asks for access to your photo library so it can show you your screenshots, find duplicates, similar shots and large videos, and delete the ones you choose.

  • All of this happens on your device. Photos and videos are never uploaded, copied to a server or seen by us.
  • To spot duplicates, the app makes a tiny fingerprint (a short code) of each photo on your device. It stays on your device.
  • Nothing is deleted unless you confirm it. Deleted items go to your phone's Recently Deleted album, where you can get them back for about 30 days.
  • You can change or remove photo access at any time in your phone's Settings.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your choices (keep, toss, later), your streak, your settings and your clean-up progress on your device, protected by your device's built-in encryption. Deleting the app removes it. If you use "Save a copy", the app creates a backup file and you choose where it goes; we never receive it.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules them on your device. They are created on your phone and are not sent from a server. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's Settings.

Widgets

Home screen widgets show your streak and progress. That information is shared only between the app and its widget on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. When paid plans launch, we may use a purchase service (RevenueCat) that receives an anonymous ID and your purchase receipt so your subscription works across reinstalls. It does not receive your name, email, photos or usage.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, ad IDs or tracking across apps and websites.
  • No photos, file names, photo details or anything you type is ever sent.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we don't have any.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which parts of the app work and which are confusing, Swipeback sends a short list of anonymous events to PostHog, our analytics provider. For example: "onboarding finished", "Rewind finished (3 kept, 2 tossed)", "cleared big videos (1–5 GB)", or "paywall shown".

  • Each install gets a random ID that isn't linked to your name, email, Apple ID or any other app. We never build a profile of you.
  • Sizes are sent as rough ranges (like "1–5 GB"), never exact numbers.
  • Nothing about your photos is sent: no images, file names, photo IDs, dates or locations.
  • We don't look up your location, and we've told PostHog to discard IP addresses.
  • It's not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.
  • Turn it off any time: Settings → Privacy → "Share anonymous usage stats". Nothing more is sent after that.

Children

Swipeback is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you. The anonymous usage statistics can't be traced back to you, but if you'd like them removed, email us and we'll help. Everything the app stores can be erased with "Reset all history" in Settings or by deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
