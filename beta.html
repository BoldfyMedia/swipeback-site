3. Good to know

  1. iPhone works best. On Android, Expo Go can't see your whole photo library, so some features are limited until the real app is out.
  2. It's safe. Nothing is deleted unless you confirm it, and deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Nothing leaves your phone. Privacy policy.
  3. Updates are automatic. Close and reopen Swipeback in Expo Go to get the newest version.
  4. Ignore any "development" labels from Expo Go. That's normal for a beta.
